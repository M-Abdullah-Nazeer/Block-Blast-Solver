## How a Block Blast Bot Solver Actually Works

Most solvers follow a simple idea:  
**scan → simulate → score → choose the best move**

Here’s what’s happening behind the scenes.

### 1. Board Detection  
The bot reads the current grid and maps out which cells are filled and which are empty.

### 2. Shape Analysis  
Each available block shape gets tested in every possible position.

### 3. Scoring the Outcome  
The solver checks what happens after placement:
- Lines cleared  
- Space left for future moves  
- Risk of locking the board  

### 4. Best Move Wins  
The move with the highest score becomes the recommendation.

Simple idea. Smart execution.
